# Changelog

## Dynamic campaign, stage 2: the campaign clock

- The campaign runs on one continuous clock. Each day the enemy flies two to four operations, and each one is a battle: a dawn probe of drones and recon, one or two combined strikes once its jets are turned round, and a night strike heavy on ballistic and cruise missiles.
- Between raids the clock stops on the planning screen. It shows a situation map (our ground, the road, located enemy installations with their remaining stock, and the expected axis of the next raid). The buttons are **Advance to the next raid** (shows the expected time) and **Wait 1 hour**.
- Intel warns of the next raid's rough time and main axis. A quarter of the time it is an hour or two out, and now and then it has the axis wrong.
- Everything carries over between raids: positions, missiles, damage to units and assets, and the enemy's fixes on our units (recon photographs and batteries located by their launches) for up to 18 hours or until the unit moves.
- Logistics take time: units on order reach the depot after 1 to 10 hours, and repair crews restore 15 points an hour. Enemy aircraft need 4 to 10 hours between sorties.
- Income and the automatic restock come at 06:00. Bonus objectives are set per raid at half the old reward and paid at once. You get one offensive and one deep strike a day. Loaned units stay until 06:00.
- After day 10 a ceasefire ends the campaign, graded Gold, Silver or Bronze by the towns held. Taking the enemy's forward airfield is still an outright win.
- An enemy operation with nothing left to fly is called off.

## Dynamic campaign, stage 1: enemy installations

- Every enemy raid and counter-battery strike launches from a real installation: a forward airfield, a helicopter arming point, drone teams, ballistic and cruise missile batteries, SAM sites and a rear air base. Each has finite stocks and a nightly trickle of reinforcements.
- An installation is located when it launches. Located installations appear on the scope, in the 3D view and in the enemy order of battle, and can be hit with deep strikes. Taking a town overruns what stands in it.
